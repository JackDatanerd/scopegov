AI draft — forced tool_choice rejected by newer models (base: b73f416)

B1  app/api/co/draft/route.ts, app/api/invoices/draft/route.ts, lib/ai/tool-call.ts (new)
    "Draft with AI" forced a tool call (tool_choice type 'tool'). With ANTHROPIC_MODEL pointed at a model that rejects
    that (400 "tool_choice: type "tool" and "any" are not supported for this model") every CO draft returned a 500.
    New createWithTool() keeps the forced call where it works; on that specific 400 it retries once with
    tool_choice 'auto' and an explicit "call the tool" instruction, and remembers the model for the instance.
    Both draft routes use it (the invoice route is hardcoded to Haiku today, so this is future-proofing).
