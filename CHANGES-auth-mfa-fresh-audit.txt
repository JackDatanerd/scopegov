Auth + MFA — fresh independent audit (baseline HEAD 7345e92 audit, fixed on a38a928)

1. HIGH   mfa/enroll: starting a FIRST enrolment now needs step-up (sign-in <=10 min or password
          grant). A hijacked password-only session could enrol its own authenticator; the enrolment's
          fresh `totp` amr then satisfied step-up (change-email, delete, transfer) and /verify's
          sign-out-others evicted the owner. UI: /mfa-setup mounts <StepUpHost/>, uses
          fetchWithStepUp, and shows a "Sign in again" state for passwordless accounts.
          middleware: /api/auth/step-up is exempt from the enrolment gate only (not the aal2 gate).
2. MEDIUM step-up (totp): refuses when there is no verified factor or factorId is not that verified
          factor (it used to be able to complete a half-enrolled factor with no codes/audit/notice).
3. LOW    Migration 115 + createStatelessAuthClient User-Agent marker: password re-verification
          sessions are no longer audited as `security.login_succeeded`.
4. LOW    forgot-password / login resend / signup resend surface 429 + failures instead of a false
          "sent" notice and cooldown.
5. LOW    middleware: GoTrue outage on getUser() -> retryable 503 (not 401 / redirect to /login).
6. LOW    safeRedirectPath: '@' and '://' only refused in the PATH (deep links with them in the query
          survive); mfa-challenge guards non-JSON responses; mfa/verify reports listFactors failure as
          verify_unavailable (502) not factor_not_found.
