Section 10 (CO logic) — independent pass fixes
CO-1  PATCH /api/co/[id]: switching a negotiated draft to credit now sheds the system-written "Negotiated discount/increase"
      lines (stripAdjustmentLines in lib/documents/co-totals.ts). Before: negative line -> 400, positive line -> silently a credit line.
      CoEditor drops those lines from its own state when the credit box is ticked.
CO-2  CoEditor: a NEW change order by a member without VIEW_FINANCIALS locks rate / tax / credit controls up front
      (GET /api/projects/[id] now returns canViewFinancials). POST /api/co already refused priced lines from them.
CO-3  POST /api/co/[id]/revise: concurrent revises no longer leave two sibling drafts — the lowest-versioned open draft wins,
      the later one deletes itself (and its copied attachment rows) and returns the winner with existing:true.
CO-4  (found while fixing CO-2) GET /api/projects/[id] did not select retainer_duration_months, so a NEW CO on a fixed-term
      retainer was treated as open-ended: the renewal-term input was hidden and Send was then refused for a missing term.
