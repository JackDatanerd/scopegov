CHANGES — documents polish: signature block, embedded fonts, bounded change-order drafts

Found by reading a signed SOW, change order and invoice side by side and checking the PDFs' fonts with pdffonts.

1. The signer's position follows the person (lib/documents/signer-title.ts, app/api/portal/sow/[token]/sign/route.ts)
   The agreement names the client's signatory and position in its body ("Faith Christine, Director"), but the signature block
   printed only what the signer typed into an optional title box, so the same person was titled in the text and untitled above
   their signature. A blank box now takes the position the agreement already names, only when the person signing is that named
   person (a colleague signing in their place never inherits it). Stored on sow_documents.signer_title at signing, so the portal
   copy, the agency copy and the executed PDF all agree. What the signer types still wins.
   Existing signed SOWs are unchanged (their stored title stays as it was).

2. Amounts use the embedded font (lib/pdf/renderer.tsx, lib/pdf/fonts.ts comment)
   Amounts, totals and the contract-position rows on the change order and invoice were set in Courier, a standard font that is NOT
   embedded in the PDF, so every viewer substituted its own face and the amounts looked different from the SOW (which has none).
   They now use the embedded Noto Sans (digits are fixed-width at 572/1000 in both weights, so right-aligned columns still line up;
   U+2212 exists too, though the ASCII minus is kept). rich-text.tsx's inline-code Courier is untouched (rare in contracts).
   Layout applies to PDFs rendered after deploy; frozen signed PDFs keep the layout they had.

3. Change-order drafts are bounded (app/api/co/draft/route.ts, prompt only)
   - Each line item states its limit (number of creators, outreach rounds, pages, posts) so added work cannot be open-ended.
   - Work listed under "Already excluded" is brought in by an explicit amendment of that exclusion, for the described work only.
   - Third-party costs (creator fees, ad spend, licences, tools) say who bears them.
   - For a retainer the draft says whether each line is one-time or monthly; an ongoing engagement with no end date gets no
     invented timeline impact.

Checked and left alone: invoice terms (payment period and late-fee notice already print while a balance is due, and are rightly
absent on a paid invoice); tax and contract-position arithmetic; PDF text layer.

tests/documents-polish.test.ts (new, 9 tests, incl. a real rendered invoice asserting no Courier). Full suite: 342 files / 3361 tests
pass; tsc clean. No migration. The CO prompt change is untested against a live model.
