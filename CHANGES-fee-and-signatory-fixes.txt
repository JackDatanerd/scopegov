CHANGES — what the amount means, and who signs, asked where it matters

The same number is a whole project's price on a fixed project and one month's fee on a retainer. The app already stored, invoiced and
printed a retainer correctly (rate x term; open-ended = months committed), but only once the project WAS a retainer, and nothing in
front of the person said which of the two they were entering. A user typing "$1,200" for a monthly deal on a Marketing project got a
$1,200 project. And a SOW with no stated price was only refused on the brief step, with "use Back to add it".

1. One sentence says what the amount means (lib/sow/billing-summary.ts, app/(app)/projects/new/page.tsx)
   Shown under the amount on Basics, again where the fee is asked on the Brief step, and on the Review step:
     fixed:   "The whole project is USD 1,200.00, invoiced as one fee or in instalments."
     monthly: "USD 1,200.00 is invoiced every month until the retainer ends. There is no fixed total."
              "USD 1,200.00 is invoiced every month for 6 months, USD 7,200.00 in total."
   With no amount it says what to enter, in the right terms. Same words everywhere the amount appears.

2. The fee is asked on the Brief step, not behind a dead end
   - An amount box (labelled Monthly retainer amount / Contract value) sits on the Brief step with the sentence above.
   - A fee the brief states ("$1,200/month") prefills it when none was set. A brief that says "monthly retainer" with NO figure
     leaves it empty and asks; the app never invents a price.
   - Accepting "Bill as a monthly retainer" also saves the brief's monthly figure when no amount was entered.
   - Continue saves the amount to the project before the Review step (the SOW is drafted from the saved project). The old
     "Use Back to add it" error is replaced by a message pointing at the box.

3. Who signs is remembered per client (app/api/clients/[id]/signatory/route.ts, lib/sow/signatory.ts)
   Picking a client fills Contracting company (from the client record) and Signer's title (the position the agreement last named for
   THAT contact; never lent to another person). Fills only empty boxes. Read-only, workspace-scoped, CREATE_PROJECTS.
   Deliberately NO migration: the position stays on each SOW's metadata. A column on clients would also have to be added to the
   create-client RPC (088) and the merge-clients function (126), which cannot be validated without a database; the route is the single
   read point, so moving it later changes one function.
