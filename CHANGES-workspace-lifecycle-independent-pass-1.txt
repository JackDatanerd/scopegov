Workspace lifecycle — independent pass 1
=========================================
Scope: api/workspace/{create,complete-onboarding,delete,leave,list,switch,profile}
(+ restore, onboarding-status, profile/avatar traced as part of the lifecycle).

FIXED
 B2/B9  delete: soft-delete + member deactivation + active-workspace reassignment are one
        transaction (delete_workspace_atomic, migration 116) under a workspace row lock.
        A second/overlapping DELETE is refused (already_deleted) instead of re-stamping
        deleted_at, which made restore match nobody. Blockers are re-checked under the lock.
        If the delete fails after the Paystack cancel, the subscription is resumed (or ops
        alerted) and the message no longer claims "nothing was changed" when billing had been.
 B10    restore_workspace_atomic and admin_restore_workspace leave members whose account was
        deleted after the workspace was deleted deactivated (they used to come back active).
 B3     sanitizeDisplayName strips bidi controls / zero-width / format characters, treats blank
        filler characters as whitespace, returns '' when nothing visible is left (callers already
        reject ''), keeps ZWNJ/ZWJ next to visible text, and never cuts an emoji in half.
 B5     restore POST: malformed body / non-UUID workspaceId -> 400 instead of 500.
 B6     Sidebar switch/leave/restore report failures instead of failing silently.
 B7     avatar upload strips EXIF/GPS/XMP/comments (JPEG) and text/EXIF/time chunks (PNG) before
        the file goes into the public bucket; EXIF orientation is preserved; unparseable images
        are refused.
 B8     Danger Zone and wizard-discard copy no longer say a deleted workspace is unrecoverable
        (it is restorable for 30 days) and list every blocker, not just signed documents.

(migration 112), slug format + active_workspace_id retry (Onboarding pass 3).
