INVOICING — independent pass (bugs only)

B1  Tax-exclusive invoices printed the GROSS total on the single (non-itemized) line item
    of the invoice PDF, above a Subtotal row showing the NET. E.g. 1,000 @ 16% "Before tax":
    line 1,160.00 / Subtotal 1,000.00 / Tax 160.00 / Amount due 1,160.00 — line + tax did not
    foot to Amount due. Shared renderer, so it hit the agency PDF download, the emailed
    attachment, and the client portal PDF.
    Fix: new lib/pdf/invoice-line.ts (invoiceSingleLineAmount); renderer.tsx uses it. Net on
    the line when tax applies and is exclusive; gross for tax-inclusive; amount when untaxed.
    Test: tests/invoice-pdf-single-line-amount.test.ts
    No migration, no data change.
