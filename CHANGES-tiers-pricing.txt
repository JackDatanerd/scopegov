TIERS & PRICING ROUND
=====================

One plan table
  lib/billing/entitlements.ts is now the only place that says what each plan is, costs and includes. PLAN_LIMITS, LIST_PRICES_USD,
  PAID_PLAN_KEYS, the homepage plans, the in-app plan cards, the calculator's PLAN_FEATURES, the custom-roles gate (route AND
  TeamClient), the integrations gate and the Team page's Solo upsell are all derived from it.

Tiers
  Solo $39    1 seat   3 projects   60 AI drafts/mo
  Starter $99 3 seats  8 projects   200   (webhooks, Zapier, API keys; one approver step)
  Pro $249    5 seats  unlimited    600   (+ custom roles, approval chains, portfolio, Slack)
  Agency $399 10 seats unlimited    1,500 (+ audit-log export)
  The AI allowances are a first guess: edit aiDraftsPerMonth in PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS.

Bug fixed: lapsed workspaces were capped like Solo
  A lapsed workspace is stored as Solo, and the invoices page, SOW page and invoice export checked planTier === 'solo' with no lapsed
  exemption: 10 rows and no export, against the Terms, FAQ and banner ("you can still view and export everything"). They now
  decide through hasFeature(plan, feature, lapsed); a lapsed workspace keeps history, invoice export, audit export and portfolio.
  Solo's last-10 history cap is removed (the fullHistory flag stays in the table).

Gates
  Approval workflows: none on Solo, one approver step on Starter, chains from Pro. Create / activate / replace-steps are gated;
  switching off, renaming and flag toggles are not, and running workflows are untouched.
  Portfolio: Pro and up (page, both APIs, sidebar, command palette). VIEW_PORTFOLIO is NOT stripped from the session: that would
  stop Starter admins editing or assigning roles that hold it.
  Audit-log export (CSV/PDF): Agency. The in-app audit log (format=json) stays open on every plan.
  roles/[id] PATCH is left ungated (roles carry no preset flag; gating it could block editing preset roles).

Monthly AI drafting allowance
  claimAiRateSlot counts sow.parseBrief / sow.generate / sow.regenerateSection / co.draft / invoice.draft per workspace per calendar
  month (UTC) against the plan's allowance; over it the call is refused (429) with the plan, the limit and the reset date, and its
  usage row is withdrawn. Guardian is deliberately NOT metered monthly (a refused check is a scope flag never raised). Fails open.
  Billing tab shows "AI drafts this month: N of M". ai_usage_log retention 30 -> 62 days so a full month is countable.
  Migration 168 adds the supporting index (safe before or after the code deploy).

Surfaces
  Pricing page and Settings plan cards render the same rows (seats, projects, Guardian, AI drafts, approvals, integrations,
  custom roles, portfolio, audit export). Integrations is named as it exists: Slack is built, HubSpot and Drive are not.
  Calculator: "full history" checkbox replaced by five needs (custom roles, approval chains, integrations, portfolio, audit export);
  old links with history=1 still load.

Not built (not claimed on any card): enforced MFA, portal branding as a paid feature, priority support, per-seat add-on,
HubSpot / Google Drive.
