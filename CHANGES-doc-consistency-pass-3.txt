Document consistency pass 3 (V6 re-audit): regressions, CO drafter, review window, client-date conflicts
==========================================================================================================

No migration (the review window reuses workspaces.default_approval_days from migration 160).

Regressions from the previous pass
- Payment Terms lost its bold "Payment period." label: the clause-level dedupe replaced the whole clause. It now keeps the lead of
  the clause it replaces.
- CO capped-quantity lines contradicted themselves ("fee per candidate contacted ... the fee covers the full quantity of 10"). Per-use
  billing wording ("per candidate contacted", "billed as used") is now removed from a line capped at its own quantity before the
  fixed-sum clause is added, and the drafting prompt forbids it.
- The SOW header's "INCL. 8.25% TAX" sat below the SIGNED badge; it now sits directly under the price label.

Change orders
- The internal project name was printed as the client's brand ("for the V6 DTC Skincare brand"). The drafter is told it is an
  internal label, and "<project name> brand/business/company" is rewritten to "the Client's brand" as a backstop.
- An activity may be said to fall under an exclusion only if the exclusion's own text names it (otherwise: additional work).
- The drafter adds only work the request names or plainly requires (no unrequested brief/report/audit lines).
- Line descriptions end consistently (no trailing full stop).

SOW
- New "Client review window" setting (business days). When set, Revision Policy states it once ("The Client will approve or request
  changes to each deliverable within N business days ..."); "if the client doesn't respond" stays a separate choice (default: Don't
  state it). With no window set, the model must say the window is agreed in writing before work begins instead of inventing a number
  or pointing at an undefined "window agreed between the parties" (and Assumptions must not just point back at Revision Policy).
- One date per client dependency: the model is told each Client-supplied item gets its own Deliverables row; a draft whose Client row
  date disagrees with an Assumptions sentence about the same thing is retried once with the conflict spelled out (the conflicting
  draft is kept if retries fail), and send validation warns about any that remain (lib/sow/date-conflicts.ts).
- The retainer overview line states the fee's tax basis to the model so "Contract value" says whether tax is included.

Documents
- One apostrophe style: letters-apostrophe-letters render curly in every PDF string (the model wrote straight, app clauses curly).
- Invoice: "Paid to date (incl. tax)" when tax applies, parallel to "Paid to date (excl. tax)" in Contract position.

Not changed on purpose: a delivery deadline for the monthly report. Nothing in the inputs gives one, and stating one is the
same invented-term problem as the old "2 business days". Table-header repeat on a continued CO table (react-pdf limitation).

Tests: tests/doc-consistency-pass-3.test.ts (new); approval-silence and doc-consistency-pass-2 updated for the intentional changes.
Full suite and tsc pass.
