Clients (section 14) — independent pass 6 (audit pass 1 of this round): 2 feature gaps closed, 5 bugs fixed.

FEATURE GAPS
G1. clients.payment_terms_note ("Net 30, wire only") was collected and editable but read by nothing. It now
    prints as a "Payment terms" block under the payment instructions on the invoice PDF (agency PDF, the emailed
    attachment and the portal PDF), on the portal invoice page, and in the invoice email and both invoice
    reminder emails (manual and automatic). Plain text everywhere — escaped in email, a text node in React.
G2. clients.timezone was editable/displayed but consumed by nothing. The client-reminders cron now judges
    "invoice coming due" on the CLIENT's calendar date (client zone -> workspace zone -> UTC) instead of UTC's.
    The query window is widened one day each side and each invoice is filtered on its own client's date.
    The edit form's hint says what the field is for. (Send hour is deliberately not gated: a daily cron that
    skipped "outside business hours" could skip a client forever.)

BUGS
B1. Client page "Recent activity" showed contact NAMES ("Contact added — Jane") to VIEW_AUDIT_LOG holders who
    lack VIEW_CLIENT_DATA (the Contacts card itself is hidden from them). Contact events are now only fetched for
    viewers who can see contacts.
B2. withPrimaryContactCc checked the MAX_CC_EMAILS cap after pushing, so a client whose CC list already held 10
    still got one contact appended (11). Checked before.
B3. A non-UUID id (mistyped URL, probe) reached Postgres (22P02) and came back as a 500 / error boundary. New
    lib/utils/uuid.ts isUuidString(); the client page, PATCH/DELETE clients/[id], both contacts routes and merge
    (source id -> 404, targetId -> 400) now answer 404/400 before querying.
B4. Contact duplicate-email check ran outside the client row lock (route-level ilike, then the RPC). Migration
    119 re-checks under the lock in client_contact_add / client_contact_update (23505 with "email" in the message,
    which the routes already map to the friendly 409). 093's cap logic and 077's body are otherwise verbatim.
B5. (a) clients list page discarded the project_members read error — an outage showed limited-access viewers 0
    projects on every client; it now throws like the detail page. (b) GET /api/clients returned
    email_bounced_at to members without VIEW_CLIENT_DATA although the page withholds it; now null.

MIGRATION: supabase/migrations/119_client_contact_email_dup_under_lock.sql — run it before/with the deploy
(the app code works without it; B4 is simply not closed until it runs).
