Document consistency pass 5 (V8 re-audit): payment terms, change-order wording, table header, warnings
=======================================================================================================

No migration.

Bugs
- Dispute Resolution read "the competent courts of State of Texas, United States" (no article). Governing Law already used
  lawWithArticle(); the dispute clause now gets the same phrase.
- The CO Impact Analysis said "This work falls outside the 'Out of Scope' section", the inverse of the meaning (excluded work
  falls UNDER that section). The drafter's tool schema and rule taught that phrase; both now say "falls under", and any "falls
  outside the Out of Scope section" is rewritten (fixOutOfScopePhrasing).

Payment Terms (English retainers) are written by the app
- Everything in the section is data (fee, tax basis, term, billing, start date, payment period, late fee, the agency's standard
  terms), and the model's version only varied in labels and order ("Payment period." / "Payment terms." / unlabeled late fee).
  retainerPaymentTermsHtml writes it: Monthly retainer fee, Retainer term, Billing, Sales tax, Payment period, Late payment,
  Suspension of work, Additional terms. Items with no data are omitted rather than invented. The agency's saved payment terms are
  kept: repeats are skipped, a suspension sentence gets its own lead. Other languages and payment structures keep the model text,
  where a bare late-fee paragraph now gets the "Late payment." lead when the rest are labelled.

Other SOW wording
- Out of Scope always ends with the change-order route ("Any of these items may be performed only under a written change order signed
  by both parties") when the model left it out.
- The model may not add Client obligations the brief never named (brand assets, extra systems, extra accounts).

Warnings
- Send validation warns when no standard review window is set and the Revision Policy defers the period ("agreed in writing before work
  begins"): a valid draft, but an unresolved term in a signed document.

Layout
- The CO line-item header row is `fixed`: react-pdf repeats it at the top of a page the table continues onto (verified in a
  5-line render: page 1 and page 2 each carry one header; a table that fits one page, and the Impact Analysis page, carry none extra).
  The SOW's short tables are still deliberately kept together on one page.

Tests: tests/doc-consistency-pass-5.test.ts (new); pass-4 wiring test updated for the dispute change. Full suite and tsc pass.
