Retainer termination: fees follow the service period, not the invoice date
==========================================================================

No migration. Found by reading a retainer SOW billed monthly in advance, whose Termination said "The Client will pay all fees invoiced
up to the effective date of termination; the fee for the monthly period in progress remains payable."

The problem: the next month's invoice is issued BEFORE that month begins. Notice given 20 Nov takes effect 30 Nov, but the December
invoice is issued around 16 Nov, so it is "invoiced up to the effective date" and, read literally, payable for a month with no service.
Every retainer billed in advance has this ambiguity, open-ended or fixed term.

lib/documents/standard-clauses.ts, terminationClauseHtml (retainer only):
- The Client pays the monthly fee for each monthly period that BEGINS on or before the effective date, including the period in progress
  when notice is given. (Begins, not ends: an agreed mid-month date still owes the month in progress.)
- No fee is payable for a period that begins after the effective date. An invoice already issued for it is cancelled and any amount
  already paid for it is refunded.
- Fees for change orders accepted, and work delivered, before the effective date stay payable, so the cancellation can never reach them.
- Effective-date rule and return/deletion of materials are unchanged. Fixed-fee termination is unchanged.

Operating note: the clause says the later invoice is cancelled. In the app that means voiding the invoice and marking the project
completed on the effective date so the monthly billing job stops.

Applies to SOWs generated from now on; signed SOWs keep their frozen text.
