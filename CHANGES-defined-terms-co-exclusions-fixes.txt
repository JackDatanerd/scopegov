SOW: no undefined "Services"; change orders: "remains excluded" must be true of the SOW
========================================================================================

No migration. Built on dfcdc91. Found by comparing regenerated documents against the first set.

1. An undefined capitalised term (lib/documents/defined-terms.ts, app/api/sow/generate/route.ts, lib/ai/sow-content.ts)
   SOW-0012 wrote "the Services" in Out of Scope, Revision Policy and Confidentiality, but its Parties clause defines only SOW, Provider
   and Client, so a capitalised term appeared in a contract with no meaning (the first SOW used lower case; the model varies run to run).
   - Drafting rule: the only defined terms are SOW, Provider and Client; write "the services".
   - Safety net after generation: unless the drafted text itself defines "Services" (e.g. (the "Services")), "the Services" / "The Services"
     in every section and table cell becomes "the services" / "The services". "The Services Company" or "Financial Services" are untouched.

2. "Remains excluded" claimed more than the SOW says (lib/documents/co-exclusions.ts, app/api/co/draft/route.ts, app/api/co/[id]/send/route.ts)
   CO-0011 said negotiating, contracting, managing "and contacting" influencers, creators or affiliate partners "remain excluded". The
   SOW's exclusion names identifying, negotiating, contracting and managing; it never names contacting. Calling it "remains" tells the
   client it was always excluded, which is a new term presented as an old one.
   - Draft prompt: say "remains excluded" only for what the SOW's Out of Scope text names; anything else this change order leaves out is
     "This change order does not include <activity>".
   - Send check: activities in a "remain(s) excluded" sentence that the signed SOW's Out of Scope text does not name raise an
     acknowledgeable warning (matched on the first five letters of the word). No SOW text to compare with: no warning.
   - findSignedSow takes { withOutOfScope: true } and returns that text, so the send route still goes through the shared lookup.

3. Not changed: the empty third of CO page 1
   The last line item travels with the Subtotal / Tax / Total (one unsplittable group) on purpose, so a page break can never strand the
   totals away from the lines they add up. When that group does not fit under a tall line, it moves to the next page whole and leaves
   a gap. That is the price of the rule, so it stays.
