BUG (low): POST /api/portal/invoice/[token]/dispute
  The per-invoice throttle (same note ignored while open, different note at most once per 15 min) read disputed_at
  from a snapshot and then wrote keyed on id alone. Simultaneous POSTs with different notes all passed the check and
  each notified up to 25 finance members (bell + email) and mailed the client a receipt. The write is now
  compare-and-set on the dispute state the request observed (disputed_at / dispute_resolved_at, null or exact);
  a loser is answered as a duplicate and notifies nobody. Same pattern as portal/invoice/[token]/paid.
  This also stops a request from overwriting a dispute the agency resolved between its read and its write.
