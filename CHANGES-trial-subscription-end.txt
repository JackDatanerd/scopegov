Trial / subscription end pass (Oct 6)

DECISION: no free Solo tier. A workspace with no subscription that nobody comped is LAPSED = read-only.
  Applies to: expired trial, non-payment downgrade (grace enforced), cancelled subscription whose paid period ended.
  Read-only keeps: viewing, exports, Billing, settings, the client portal (clients mid-signature/payment are unaffected).
  Read-only drops: every writing permission (create/edit/send SOW, CO, invoices, projects, invites, approvals, roles, delete).
  Known consequence: SEND_INVOICES also covers recording payments, so a lapsed workspace cannot record one by hand.

MIGRATION 150 (run it): workspaces.lapsed_at. Backfill only unambiguous ex-trials (Solo + past trial_ends_at + no live
  subscription). Earlier non-payment/cancelled Solo workspaces are grandfathered (indistinguishable from staff comps).
