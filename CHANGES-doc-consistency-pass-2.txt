Document consistency pass 2: retainer SOW / CO / invoice harmonization
======================================================================

Invoices
- A monthly-retainer milestone's date is the first day of the month it pays for (the billing-month anchor), not a payment
  deadline. Creating an invoice from one used that date as the due date, so an invoice issued Oct 13 fell due Nov 1 whatever the
  SOW's "within N days of invoice date" said. With a standard term set in Settings the due date now comes from the term (still
  editable before sending); other milestones keep the existing "later of milestone date and term" rule.
- The retainer line no longer repeats itself ("Monthly retainer - November 2026" / "Monthly retainer payment - November 2026"):
  the grey sub-line states the service period (November 1 - 30, 2026). A trigger that only repeats the title is dropped.
  Same on the client portal invoice page (lib/pdf/invoice-line.ts: invoiceLineSubtext).

SOW
- Payment Terms: "Invoices are due 14 days of issue; each invoice is payable within 14 days of its invoice date." The dedupe
  worked per sentence and a semicolon joined the two statements; it now works per clause and keeps one well-formed sentence
  (capitalised). The drafting prompt also asks for the period once.
- Payment Schedule trigger for retainers: "Monthly - first of month" read as a due date and contradicted net-N terms; it now says
  "Invoiced monthly, in advance" (all six languages).
- The header states the tax basis ("incl. 8.25% tax") for retainers too; it was suppressed for them.
- Signed SOWs are not edited: a change order names the SOW version it amends instead.

Change orders
- "(General scope)" leaked into the client-facing Impact Analysis: it is the Guardian flag's internal fallback label, passed to
  the drafter as "SOW reference this exceeds". It is no longer passed as a reference, the model is told never to print a label
  the SOW does not print, and any echoed "(General scope)" is stripped (lib/documents/co-draft-text.ts).
- The drafter is told to describe an exclusion only in its own words and, when the request goes beyond the exclusion's text
  (e.g. affiliate partners vs "influencers, creators, brand ambassadors"), to say the CO adds that work as additional work and
  amends the exclusion only for the part it covers.
- A per-unit line capped at its own quantity ("up to 10 candidates", qty 10) now states that the fee covers the full quantity
  whether or not all are used (ensureCappedQuantityBasis); the agency can edit the line.
- "Reason for Change" is drafted as a reason (the Client's request, then what the Provider will do and who bears third-party
  costs) rather than a restatement of the line items.
- The last line item travels with Subtotal / Tax / Total (no orphaned totals on the next page).
- Tax-inclusive COs say "Rates and line totals include tax."; the retainer one-time fee also shows "Including tax (8.25%)".
- "Amends SOW No. SOW-0007 · Version 1" (CO header; PDF route, portal PDF route and the executed copy all pass the version).

Tests: tests/doc-consistency-pass-2.test.ts (new); retainer-change-orders and co-independent-pass-10 updated for the intentional changes.
No migration.

Not in this tarball (need a decision or a larger change): Limitation of liability as its own numbered SOW section; a deemed-approval
rule for client review; a client-facing document title separate from the internal project name; delivery dates / assumptions on COs.
