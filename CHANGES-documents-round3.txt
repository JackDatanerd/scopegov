Documents, independent pass 3

1. Sending a non-English SOW no longer erases its frozen payment period. The text reader only overrides the frozen value when it can
   read the text: another language, or an English day count it cannot interpret, keeps what was frozen. It also reads "Net N". An
   English text that mentions no day count still drops the period. (lib/documents/payment-terms.ts: readPaymentTerms, reconcile)
2. Title Case is applied to English documents only (toTitleCaseFor). Invoice PDFs now carry the SOW's language (invoice-refs,
   pdf/invoice, portal invoice pdf, send-invoice) so a Spanish/French/Swahili invoice keeps its titles as written.
3. Deliverables coverage check reads accented words whole and says nothing for a SOW drafted in another language.
4. The invoice modal's default due date is the signed SOW's period whenever the SOW states one (billingDefaults.termFromSow); a
   milestone's own later date no longer produces a default that sending refuses. Workspaces with no SOW term keep the old rule.
Tests: tests/documents-gap-closure.test.ts (es/de/sw cases); late-fee.test.ts expects the new `language` field.
