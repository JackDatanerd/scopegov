Change orders: delivery before project start, undefined revisions; document polish; one prompt conflict from the clauses pass
==========================================================================================================================

No migration. Found by reading a retainer starting 1 November with a one-time change order due 26 October, whose shortlist line said
what the limit was but not what a shortlist contains or what happens to changes.

1. Delivery date before the project starts (lib/documents/co-delivery.ts, app/api/co/[id]/send/route.ts)
   Sending a charged change order whose delivery date is earlier than the project's start date now raises an acknowledgeable warning
   naming both dates. It may be intended (pre-start work), but nothing in the documents explained it. Silent for credits, monthly-rate
   changes, unpriced orders and when either date is missing. The send route now also loads projects.start_date and the CO note.

2. Change order that delivers a work product but never says what happens to changes (lib/documents/co-definition.ts)
   The SOW's revision policy was written for the original deliverables, so it does not obviously cover a new list, report, plan or
   design. A charged change order whose line delivers one of those, and whose note, scope impact and lines never mention revisions or
   changes, raises an acknowledgeable warning at send. Deliberately narrow: hours, posts and other repeated units are governed by the
   SOW as written, so there is no warning on every priced change order.
   AI draft (app/api/co/draft/route.ts): a line that delivers a list, report, brief or plan says what each entry or part contains;
   scopeImpact states whether the new work includes revisions, or that changes need a further change order.

3. Polish
   - Change order: "Monthly retainer (unchanged) (excl. tax)" is now "Monthly retainer, unchanged (excl. tax)".
   - Paid invoice: "Paid in full" appeared three times (badge, totals, footer). The footer line is gone on a paid invoice; the badge
     and the totals row remain. Unpaid invoices keep their footer.
   - SOW drafting rules: a brand, product or account the brief names that is not the Client's own name is introduced once as the
     Client's ("the Client's Solvane brand"); when revisions cover only some things (captions), the Revision Policy says anything else
     (edits to cuts) is not covered and needs a change order.

4. Correction to the previous tarball (lib/ai/sow-content.ts)
   The "view-only access to report data" drafting rule added last time contradicted an older rule saying never to add Client
   obligations such as extra systems. The model could follow the older one and drop it. The older rule now carries the exception.

Left alone on purpose: the "fee covers the full quantity of N whether or not all are used" wording in a change-order line. It is
required by an existing rule (ensureCappedQuantityBasis) so a capped line carries its own billing basis wherever it is reprinted.
Not changed: who owns a delivered shortlist (the SOW's IP clause names captions and edited content only); a business term to decide.
