App-written Payment Terms for English fixed-fee SOWs (upfront / 50-50 / on delivery)
=====================================================================================

No migration. Built on top of the "Documents close-out" commit (b505b4d); that commit was reviewed with the rest (tsc clean, full
suite green) and left untouched.

Why: pass 5 made the English retainer Payment Terms app-written because every fact in the section is data and the model's version
only varied in labels and order. Fixed-fee SOWs, the most common kind, still had that variance. fixedFeePaymentTermsHtml does the
same for them: Fee, Payment structure (upfront / 50-50 / on delivery, with the start date when there is one), Sales tax (when it
applies, inclusive or exclusive), Payment period, Late payment, then the agency's own standard terms (a suspension sentence gets
its own lead, repeats are skipped). Items with no data are omitted, never invented.

The shared tail (tax, payment period, late fee, agency terms) is one helper used by both versions, so retainer output is unchanged.
Milestone payments (their schedule is authored per project) and non-English SOWs keep the model-written section.

Tests: tests/doc-consistency-pass-6.test.ts (new); the pass-5 wiring test follows the new generator line. Full suite and tsc pass.
