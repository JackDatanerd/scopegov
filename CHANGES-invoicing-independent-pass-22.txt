CHANGES — Section 12 Invoicing, independent pass 22 (fresh audit)

Scope audited: api/invoices/*, components/invoices/BillingTab.tsx, app/(app)/invoices, api/pdf/invoice, plus the lib code
and DB functions/triggers behind them. One bug found.

B1 (low) — invoice CSV export dated Issued / Voided-on in UTC
  app/api/invoices/export/route.ts printed String(sent_at).slice(0, 10) and String(voided_at).slice(0, 10), the UTC date of a
  real instant. A workspace east of UTC (e.g. Nairobi, UTC+3) saw an invoice sent 00:00–03:00 local dated the previous day in the
  CSV while the invoice PDF showed the right day. Issued, Voided on and the file-name date now use the workspace timezone
  (getWorkspaceTimeZone + isoDateInZone, same helpers as the other CSV exports). Paid on is intentionally unchanged: paid_at is
  the date-received date stored as UTC midnight (migration 125), so converting it would shift it a day west of UTC.
