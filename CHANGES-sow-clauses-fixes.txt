SOW clauses: client-supplied materials, data access for reports, supply shortfall, review window against cadence
===============================================================================================================

Migration 163 (default_client_materials_terms). Found by reading a retainer where the Client supplies UGC footage and the Provider
reports Shopify traffic, 6 posts a week, with 7 business days to review each post.

1. Client-supplied materials terms (opt-in; Settings -> Client-supplied materials)
   The SOW said the Client keeps ownership of its footage but never that it holds the rights and consents to have it edited and
   published, and gave the Provider no licence to use it. The drafting prompt forbids the model from writing any warranty or
   indemnity, so it could not appear at all. lib/documents/client-materials.ts now owns two fixed sentences, applied only when the
   workspace chooses "standard", English only, frozen into the SOW metadata:
   - Intellectual Property: the Client confirms it owns or has the rights, licences, consents and releases (including from anyone
     who appears in or created the materials), is responsible for third-party claims that the materials as supplied infringe, and
     licenses the Provider to use them to perform the services. No indemnity wording.
   - Assumptions, retainers only: if materials a recurring deliverable depends on do not arrive in time, the Provider delivers what
     the supplied materials allow, the monthly fee is unchanged, and the shortfall is not carried over or credited unless agreed in
     writing. (Closes "ongoing footage, as needed" having no consequence.)
   Both are idempotent and are never added when the setting is off.

2. A report on data the Provider does not control needs access to that data
   A SOW promised "social-to-Shopify traffic referrals" while the Client's dependencies listed only TikTok and Instagram access and
   Shopify management was excluded, so the report could not be produced as written.
   - Drafting: when the brief promises a report on traffic, referrals, sales, conversions or analytics, the model is told to add a
     Roles row and an Assumption for view-only access (or a regular export), due with the account access, and to say it is not
     administration of any store or system.
   - Send: a SOW that promises such a report without stating that access raises an acknowledgeable warning. Never a block.

3. Review window against cadence (send warning)
   More than 5 business days to review, with a weekly or daily deliverable, now raises an acknowledgeable warning: reviews would
   outlast the cadence, so work piles up or goes out late. Never a block.
