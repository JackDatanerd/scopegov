Payment Terms: no repeated due-days sentence, no conflicting late fee, and a soft warning for a high late fee
==============================================================================================================

No migration. Found by reading a retainer SOW whose Payment Terms said "Payment period. Each invoice is due within 14 days of its
invoice date." and then, two paragraphs later, "Additional terms. Invoices are due 14 days of issue."

1. Repeated, badly worded payment period (lib/ai/sow-content.ts)
   The app now writes the retainer and fixed-fee Payment Terms itself (retainerPaymentTermsHtml / fixedFeePaymentTermsHtml). That
   happens AFTER dedupePaymentPeriodSentences runs on the model's text, and the shared tail only skipped agency-standard sentences
   that were exact substrings of what was already written. A standard written in other words slipped through as "Additional terms".
   - restatesPaymentPeriod(): recognises "Invoices are due 14 days of issue", "Payment is due within 14 days", "Net 14" in any
     wording. Sentences about suspension or late payment that only mention the same number of days are NOT treated as restating it.
   - statesLateFeePercent(): an agency-standard sentence giving a late-fee percentage is not added when the SOW already states the
     workspace late fee, so one SOW can never carry two different rates.
   - Both apply only when the SOW writes its own payment period / late fee. With no period set, the agency's sentence stays.

2. Soft warning for a high late fee (lib/documents/late-fee.ts, lib/sow/validate-send.ts, components/settings/SettingsClient.tsx)
   The late-fee field accepts up to 100% a month. Above 1.5% a month (18% a year) a note now shows under the Settings field, and the
   SOW send dialog lists it as an acknowledgeable warning. It never blocks. Example: "2.7% per month is about 32.4% a year". The
   contract sentence still limits the fee to the legal maximum. This is a prompt to check, not legal advice.

tests/payment-terms-fixes.test.ts (26 new). Checked: tsc, next lint (0 errors), full suite 356 files / 3565 tests.
Already-signed SOWs keep their frozen text. Only SOWs generated from now on change.
