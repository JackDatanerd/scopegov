B1 (MEDIUM) app/api/billing/webhook/route.ts  (subscription.not_renew / subscription.disable)
   billing/cancel refuses to flag a cancellation against a missing or already-elapsed current_period_end
   (passes 7/8), but the webhook handler — the path a cancellation made through Paystack's own manage
   link/email arrives by — set cancels_at_period_end and left the date alone. An elapsed date (renewal not
   yet refreshed) let cron/payment-overdue step 5 downgrade a customer who had just paid another period; a
   NULL date is never selected by step 5, so the customer kept a paid plan indefinitely. The handler now
   takes a FUTURE next_payment_date from the event, else from Paystack (best-effort), and writes it in the
   same compare-and-set update as the flag, only when the stored date is missing/elapsed. The audit row's
   ends_at uses the refreshed date.
