  delete -> restore therefore un-cancelled the plan and the customer was charged at the next cycle.
  - migration 132: billing.cancelled_by_workspace_delete_at (+ backfill for workspaces deleted inside
    the 30-day restore window whose delete cancelled billing and where no earlier cancellation existed).
  - workspace/delete: sets the marker only when IT cancelled (not when already non-renewing), clears a

    success; keeps the marker and pages billing ops if the resume fails.
  Workspaces deleted BEFORE 132 that the backfill cannot classify will not auto-resume on restore
  (the safe direction); billing ops can resume those by hand.
