scopegov — Section 14 (Clients) — independent pass 15 fixes
===========================================================
Cold independent audit of api/clients, components/clients, app/(app)/clients (plus the traced
webhook). Applied against the live snapshot taken after the pass-15 audit; none of the files
other sessions were touching (guardian, projects dashboard, scope-governance) are modified here.
No migration required. No new dependencies.

B1  lib/utils/client-input.ts
    EMAIL_RE was a pure deny-list and the domain's list never covered ordinary punctuation, so
    addresses carrying paste artefacts passed create / edit / CC / contact validation and then
    bounced on the first send: jane@acme.com'  'jane@acme.com'  jane@acme.com…
    “jane@acme.com”  jane@acme.com?subject=hi  jane@acme.com»  jane@ac!me.com  a@ex%ample.com
    a@x.com/  a@x.com|  jane@acme.com€ …  Two lookaheads were added to the existing pattern: the
    DOMAIN rejects any Unicode punctuation / symbol / other-category character except . - and
    ZWNJ / ZWJ / U+00B7 (kept: real in some scripts' domain names); the LOCAL part keeps every
    ASCII atext special (+ ' * ! # $ % & / = ? ^ _ ` { | } ~ -) but refuses NON-ASCII punctuation
    (curly quotes, ellipsis, guillemets) — a paste artefact, never a typed address.
    The existing deny-list is kept (a positive \p{L} class would have re-admitted the Hangul
    filler characters, which are letters). Linear-time on hostile input; all earlier pass
    10 / 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 rejections and acceptances still hold.

B2  app/api/webhooks/resend/route.ts   (traced out of Clients)
    trackClientEmailHealth matched the client with ilike(escapeLike(to)) alone in all three
    branches. escapeLike() maps a "*" to the "_" single-character wildcard (PostgREST cannot
    escape "*", and "*" is a legal local-part character), so an event for a*@x.com also matched
    ab@x.com / a1@x.com: a bounce or spam complaint stamped the red marker on an UNRELATED client,
    and a delivery cleared another client's genuine bounce. The pass-13 fix (exact sameEmail()
    after the LIKE) had been applied to contacts, project creation and Guardian inbound but not
    here. Candidates are now read (limit 25), filtered with sameEmail(), and written by id.
    Cost: one small select before the update on "delivered" events.

B3  lib/reports/client-money.ts (new), app/(app)/clients/[id]/page.tsx
    The Money block mixed tax bases: Contracted / Invoiced came from computeContractPositions()
    (deliberately PRE-tax) while Paid / Outstanding / Overdue are POST-tax, so a fully settled
    client billed with tax read  Invoiced 1,000 · Paid 1,160 · Outstanding 0.
    DECISION: Invoiced is now the invoice TOTAL (incl. tax) — Σ amount of non-draft, non-void
    invoices — so Invoiced, Paid and Outstanding share one basis and Invoiced − Paid = Outstanding
    again. Contracted stays ex-tax (it is what was scoped). Each card now says its basis
    ("· ex. tax" / "· incl. tax") and a one-line caption explains it.
    computeContractPositions() is NOT changed — the invoice PDFs' "Remaining contract" needs the
    pre-tax figure. The page's two invoice reads (positions + open invoices) are unchanged in
    count: the open-invoices read became one non-draft read that feeds Invoiced, Outstanding and
    Overdue from a single consistent set. Arithmetic lives in a pure helper (summarizeClientMoney)
    so it is unit-tested; sums are rounded to cents; a failed read still blanks the whole summary.
